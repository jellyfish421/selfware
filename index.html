Why the alternatives don't cut it
vs. Spreadsheets

Static. Break under real workflows. Require constant manual updates. Someone always has to be the glue holding it together, and that someone is already too busy.

vs. Airtable / Notion

Great for simple tracking. Falls apart when your workflow needs logic, automation, and multiple connected views. They're documents pretending to be systems.

vs. Off-the-shelf SaaS

Built for everyone means optimized for no one. You spend months configuring it, pay monthly forever, and still end up with workarounds for the parts it doesn't fit.

vs. Zapier automations

Automations on top of broken systems are still broken. They need maintenance, break without warning, and don't fix the underlying data problem.
